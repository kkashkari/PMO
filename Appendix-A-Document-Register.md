## Conventions

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ID** | `D-<step>-<seq>`. Steps not present in the JSON use the phase number plus `X` (e.g. `D-2X`) — these are statutory gaps identified in HLD §4.5. |
| **G/O** | **G** = generated by the entity · **O** = obtained from an external party · **G/O** = entity form completed and signed by both |
| **Owner** | Accountable role (process definition role key where one exists) |
| **Basis** | Article of the Executive Regulations unless prefixed `Law` |
| **Artefact** | Achieve DocType or attachment slot that holds it |

**Mandatory metadata on every document:** Hijri + Gregorian date, issuing authority, version, related tender/contract, e-signature or wet-signature scan, retention class, confidentiality class (Public / Internal / Restricted / Sealed).

**Retention classes:** `R1` publication evidence — 5 yrs · `R2` tender file — 10 yrs from award · `R3` contract file — 10 yrs from final handover (decennial liability, Art. 99) · `R4` permanent (committee formation decisions, delegations, debarment) · `LH` legal hold overrides all.
